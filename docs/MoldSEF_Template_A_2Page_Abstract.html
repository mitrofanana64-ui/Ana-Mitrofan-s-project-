1. Problem Definition & Socio-Educational Context

In the Republic of Moldova, over 18,000 high school seniors sit the National Baccalaureate Exam (administered by ANCE) annually in Romanian Language & Literature and Romanian & Universal History. The ANCE 100-point examination requires structured essays, comparative historical source analysis, and functional texts governed by strict regulatory rules—such as Article 59, which invalidates any exam paper containing a personal signature instead of the designated fictional persona in Subject III.

Students and educators face two systemic barriers: (1) Economic Inequality: Private BAC tutoring in Moldova averages 250 MDL (≈13.00 EUR) per session; preparing for two subjects across a 36-week school year costs 18,000 MDL (≈935 EUR) per student annually, exceeding an average monthly net salary and disadvantaging rural students. (2) Methodological Failure of Existing Graders: Heuristic keyword matchers (Algorithm A) fail on valid Romanian synonyms and are trivially fooled by adversarial keyword-stuffing (scoring >90% on incoherent lists of buzzwords), while unconstrained zero-shot semantic models (Algorithm B) over-score fluent off-topic prose and ignore deterministic ANCE rules.

2. Testable Scientific Hypothesis (X → Y by Z%)

Hypothesis (H1): Coupling a multi-representation Romanian semantic vector space (64-dim Latent Concept Ontology + 256-dim FastText Subword 3-Grams + IDF-weighted Corpus TF-IDF) with per-criterion ANCE rubric decomposition and a quantitative Syntactic Coherence Gate (Algorithm C — Bac.ai v2 Hybrid) reduces grading Mean Absolute Error (MAE) relative to blind human BAC teacher scores by >40% on real, unscripted 12th-grade classroom responses (Nclassroom = 30) and by >65% on stratified stress-test trials (Nsynthetic = 120), while reducing keyword-stuffing false-positive scores by >80 percentage points at sub-10 ms local CPU latency.

3. Mathematical Formulation & Multi-Algorithm Architecture

For a student response Ts, reference model answer Tm, and decomposed ANCE rubric criteria {C1, &dots;, CK}, Bac.ai v2 (src/ai/nlpEngine.js) executes a 5-stage pipeline: (1) Romanian diacritic normalization and Snowball inflectional stemming (Porter, 2001); (2) Multi-representation cosine similarity projection:

Ssem(u, v) = 0.52 · cos(econcept(u), econcept(v)) + 0.30 · cos(etfidf(u), etfidf(v)) + 0.18 · cos(e3gram(u), e3gram(v))

where econcept ∈ ℜ64 encodes 64 domain-specific synonym clusters, etfidf applies sublinear TF (1 + ln tf) and smoothed corpus IDF (Salton & Buckley, 1988), and e3gram ∈ ℜ256 applies signed character trigram hashing (Bojanowski et al., 2017); (3) A Syntactic Coherence Gate measuring Carroll's (1964) Corrected Type-Token Ratio (CTTR = |Vunique| / √(2N)), functional grammar ratio rfunc ∈ [0.20, 0.62], and Romanian verbal predicate density; and (4) Target elaboration completeness scaling and deterministic ANCE Art. 59 persona verification.

4. Dual Experimental Validation: Classroom Field Study (N = 30) & Stress-Test (N = 120)

We evaluated Algorithms A, B, and C across Ntotal = 150 trials (node scripts/run_benchmark.js): (1) Real-World 12th-Grade Classroom Field Validation (Nclassroom = 30, classroomValidationData.js): 30 unscripted practice BAC responses written by 12th-grade classmates across all 18 official items, independently and blindly graded by two BAC teachers (Inter-Teacher Blind Agreement: Pearson r = 0.9711). (2) Controlled Stratified & Adversarial Stress-Test (Nsynthetic = 120, benchmarkDataset.js): 120 trials across 6 behavioral categories.

5. Economic Benefit (ROI) & Responsible AI Governance

Economic Impact in Moldova: Replacing private tutoring (2 subjects × 36 weeks × 250 MDL = 18,000 MDL / 935 EUR per student/year) with local CPU execution (0 MDL API cost; ~350 MDL / 18 EUR amortized school hosting/year) yields net savings of 17,650 MDL (917 EUR) per student annually (98.1% cost reduction), or 529,500 MDL (27,506 EUR)/year for a classroom of 30 students. Teacher grading time drops from 22 minutes to 5.75 ms AI + 3 minutes Human-in-the-Loop review (86.4% time saved / 76 hours/year). All student data is processed 100% locally and anonymized in SQLite (database.sqlite) in compliance with Moldova Law No. 133/2011 and GDPR.
